# Brief — RTA competition

Every LLM stage (cards, generation, critique, judging) reads this file. Edit it as you
learn more; the more specific it is, the less generic the ideas.

## Who we pitch to

- **RTA** — Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority: roads, traffic, Metro, Tram, buses,
  taxis, marine transport, shared mobility, parking, licensing.
- Judges are mostly **non-technical** (officials, innovation managers, possibly investors).
  An idea must make sense in one sentence and impress on first hearing. Technical depth
  belongs in the demo and Q&A, not the hook.

## What RTA cares about (from our topic notes)

- Intelligent Traffic Systems and C-ITS rolling out across Dubai's main roads.
- Soft-mobility plan: walking/cycling/e-scooter links between homes and **63 public
  transport stations** (crossings, parking, pick-up/drop-off).
- Cycling and e-scooter use growing fast.
- **Autonomous taxis already operating**; **Dubai Mobility Labs** tests AV / future
  mobility across **248 scenarios**.
- Strategic objectives: zero-emission mobility, sustainability, operational efficiency.
- Ambition to be a **data-driven organisation** with strong customer insight.
- Dubai-specific conditions: extreme heat, sand and sandstorms, flash floods,
  outdoor workers, very mixed multilingual population, tourists, big events.

## Our constraints

- **Simulation-first.** The competition deliverable should be demonstrable in software
  (simulators, digital twins, synthetic data, recorded/public datasets, maps).
  Hardware is fine as a **later phase** of the roadmap, not for the first demo.
- The demo must be buildable by a small team within the competition timeline.
- Prefer ideas where a 2-minute simulated demo produces a visible "wow" moment
  (before/after, a near-miss avoided, a queue that disappears, a map that turns green).

## What wins (use as the tie-breaker everywhere)

Big recognisable Dubai problem → simple powerful concept → technology quietly makes it
possible → clear measurable impact (minutes saved, lives/injuries, AED, CO₂, % of people
reached).
